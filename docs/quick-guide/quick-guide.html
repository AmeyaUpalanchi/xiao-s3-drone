1 Motors (plug into M1 to M4)

M4 · GPIO 1 · CCWblack / white wire motor
M1 · GPIO 4 · CWred / blue wire motor
M3 · GPIO 3 · CWred / blue wire motor
M2 · GPIO 44 · CCWblack / white wire motor
PropsM1 + M3: CW propsM2 + M4: CCW props

720, 716 or 8520 coreless motors on 2-pin JST-SH 1.0 plugs. Diagonal pairs spin the same way, matching the arrows printed next to each plug. If a motor turns the wrong way, swap its two wires. Then check every motor with Motor test in the app (gear button).

2 Battery

1S LiPo, 3.7 V, high-C (e.g. 300 to 600 mAh). Solder an XT30 or short leads to BT+ / BT−.

Reverse-polarity protected (MOSFET + TVS), but still check + and − before the first plug-in.

Charge it off the drone with a 1S charger. The XIAO's USB-C only powers the XIAO for flashing; motors and arm lights stay off.

The app shows battery % and minutes left, warns at 25 % and 15 %, and the drone lands itself when the cell stays under 3.2 V. Land at about 3.5 V. Unplug after flying: the 5 V boost and arm lights keep drawing current.

4 First flight in 8 steps

  1. Props off. Fit the XIAO with USB-C to the front and the MPU-6050 module on its header.
  2. Plug the motors in exactly as in diagram 1.
  3. Connect the battery and set the drone down level. Hands off until the orange LED blinks every 2 s.
  4. Open the app and tap Connect. Allow the drone's Wi-Fi when Android asks.
  5. Gear → Motor test: right motor, right direction? Then Level calibration on a flat table.
  6. Props on: M1 + M3 CW, M2 + M4 CCW (front right + rear left are CW).
  7. Let go of the left stick, tap Arm, push the left stick up slowly. Hover low over grass.
  8. Land with the stick or Land, then Disarm. Hold to stop cuts the motors at once. If Wi-Fi drops for 0.5 s the drone lands itself.

5 Program it (ESP-IDF)

  1. Install ESP-IDF 5.0 (Espressif installer, or ~/esp/esp-idf).
  2. Open the project's firmware/ folder in a terminal and run . ~/esp/esp-idf/export.sh.
  3. idf.py set-target esp32s3, then idf.py build.
  4. Plug in the XIAO's USB-C. No port? Hold BOOT while plugging in.
  5. idf.py -p PORT flash monitor. The log narrates the boot.

No toolchain? Flash TheDroneCommunity-firmware-…-full.bin at 0x0 with the release's flash script or esptool-js in Chrome. After that, update over Wi-Fi from the app.

Change things in idf.py menuconfig → ESPDrone Config: motor pins, camera, Wi-Fi channel. Wi-Fi name, password and level trim live in the app.

Other ways in: http://192.168.43.42/status and /api/…, CRTP over UDP port 2390, camera at :81/stream.

6 What it can do

  • XIAO ESP32-S3: 2 cores at 240 MHz, Wi-Fi + Bluetooth LE, 8 MB flash, 8 MB PSRAM.
  • MPU-6050 6-axis IMU on I²C (GPIO 5/6), read at 1 kHz.
  • 4 brushed motor outputs, MOSFET + flyback diode each, 15 kHz PWM. For 720, 716 and 8520 motors on 1S.
  • 1 kHz stabiliser from the Crazyflie / ESP-Drone firmware.
  • Android app: arm, auto-land, hold-to-stop, flying styles, battery % and minutes left.
  • Camera with the XIAO ESP32-S3 Sense: live video, photos, MP4 recording.
  • Height hold with a VL53L1X on a Qwiic port, plus a Take off button.
  • Wireless firmware updates, own Wi-Fi name and password, motor test, find my drone.
  • Not on board: barometer, GPS, optical flow. So no position hold.

7 Something wrong?

Keeps rebootingMPU-6050 not found. Reseat the module; its pins must match the labels.
No drone Wi-FiWait 5 s after power-on while it picks a quiet channel. Custom password? Enter it under gear → Wi-Fi.
Won't armLet go of the left stick. Is the app connected?
Drifts in hoverLevel calibration on a flat table, then fine-trim in stick settings.
Flips on take-offWrong motor slot, spin or prop. Run Motor test, recheck diagram 1.
Motor 2 is deadIt's on GPIO 44 (D7). Wired to D8 instead? Set MOTOR02_PIN to 7.
Resets at full throttlePack sagging: high-C pack, bulk capacitor, check for worn motors.
Disarms by itselfBy design: 20 s idle on the ground, or the app disconnected.

8 Upgrades

  • XIAO ESP32-S3 Sense camera board: FPV video, photos and recording in the app.
  • VL53L1X time-of-flight sensor on a Qwiic port: altitude hold and Take off.
  • Bulk capacitor (470 µF low-ESR) across BT+ / BT−: steadier power at full throttle.
  • More I²C sensors on the second Qwiic port. Spare XIAO pins: D6, D8, D9, D10.
  • 8520 motors or 65 mm props for more thrust and a lower hover throttle.
  • Your own code: everything is open. Start in menuconfig and flight_safety.c.

9 Safety

  • Props off for every bench test, motor test and firmware update.
  • Never charge unattended or charge a puffed pack. Charge off the drone.
  • Fly away from faces, people and pets. Keep a thumb near Hold to stop.
  • Store LiPos at about 3.8 V, unplugged, in a fire-safe bag.
  • The default password 12345678 is public. Change it under gear → Wi-Fi before flying near others.
